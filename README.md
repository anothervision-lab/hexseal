## The second door

If **hexseal.net** does not answer, the money path of your deals is still open
from here. It asks nothing of our servers: it reads the chain through public
nodes and signs with your own wallet and your own gas.

**→ https://anothervision-lab.github.io/hexseal/**

It shows the deals the chain holds for your address, the amount and terms of
each, the clocks that are actually running, and every money door of a deal —
each one checked against the chain before the page offers it. There is no chat, no
publishing, no profiles and no arbiter panel in it: this is the money path and
nothing else.

`hexseal-second-door.html` on that page is the same door as **one file**, to keep
or to hand to somebody else. Opened from a disk it **reads** everything and
**signs** nothing — a browser keeps wallet extensions out of file addresses — and
the page says so itself and offers the two ways out.

### Check what you are opening

A static copy can be hosted by anybody, and **nothing inside the artefact proves
it is ours.** Two things do, and both are outside the reach of whoever copied it:

1. **This address.** Only the owner of this repository can publish to it.
2. **The sums below**, which live in this file's own git history. The door prints
   its build id on its own screen; `HASHES.txt` beside it repeats these.

chain and you can check them anywhere:

⚠️ **One press on that page signs a bearer document** — a file that carries out
one action in your name until its short window shuts, so that somebody else can
pay the gas for it. Whoever holds that file can send it. The page warns you first
and the window is deliberately short, and your next ordinary press cancels it.
Do not produce one for anybody you would not have pressed the button for
yourself.
